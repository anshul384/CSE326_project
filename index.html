How to use MentorMatch

  1. Mentee: open Find Mentors, compare match scores and send an invitation.
  2. Mentor: open Invitations and accept or decline requests.
  3. After acceptance, both sides can log meetings and give feedback.
  4. Admin: monitors users, pairings, meetings and ratings.

Match score = 50% interests + 20% goals + 30% availability.
